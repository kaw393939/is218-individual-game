# Research overview

**November 5, 2026 · Research & planning**

How can a solo developer use AI to make a small game they understand and can maintain? This notebook collects evidence before choosing a concept, engine, and workflow.

| Topic | Question |
| --- | --- |

## Key findings

- **Choose the game before the engine.** Compare [Phaser and Godot](./engines.md) against one small 2D concept.
- **REA supports focused investigations.** Read the [game reconstruction guide](./rea-game-reconstruction.md) for published examples and our BG3/Cyberpunk inspection results.
- **Measure the complete effort.** Count review, corrections, and playtesting alongside the first AI draft; productivity remains unmeasured here.
